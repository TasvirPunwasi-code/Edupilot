Welcome to EduPilot

EduPilot is a simple and efficient school management platform designed to help educational institutions manage student information in an organized and convenient way.

EduPilot makes it easier to manage student records, teachers, subjects, marks, academic reports, events, and performance analytics from one centralized platform.

This project was developed with the goal of creating a user-friendly system that saves time, reduces paperwork, and improves the management of academic information.

📚 Easy Management

Manage student records, teachers, subjects, marks, and academic information easily.

🔒 Secure

Keep important student and school information organized and protected.

⚡ Efficient

Reduce manual work and improve administrative efficiency.
